PRIVACY

Respect for your
information.

Application information is used to review applications, conduct selection, contact applicants, arrange interviews, and coordinate participation. Google Forms and Google Sheets are used; only authorized recruitment members may view submissions. Information is not sold or provided to unrelated third parties except where required by law, and applicant information must not be entered into external AI services without permission.

Unsuccessful applicants: delete within six months after selection. Withdrawn applicants: delete in principle within 30 days. Participant information is retained only as needed. Requests for deletion or correction are accepted by email.
